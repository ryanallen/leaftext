# The Saints Who Meet Arthur

Written in Latin, 1100 CE. Dated about 1100 in Wikipedia's "List of Arthurian literature"; the journal edition gives no secure composition date (introduction, pp. 279–281; historical discussion, pp. 297–312).

The text is the work itself.

La Borderie, Arthur de, ed. Saint Efflam: Texte inédit de la vie ancienne de ce saint avec notes et commentaire historique. Annales de Bretagne 7 (1892): 279–312. Latin text, 282–296.

Free to use: Printed in Rennes in 1892, before 1931, so free in the United States. The retained PDF is assembled from the public journal images, pp. 279–312 plus the volume cover. The Gallica offprint was inaccessible and isn't the base witness. OCR is a new raw machine reading, not established text.
