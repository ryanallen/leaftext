# The History of the Kings of Britain

Written in Latin, 1136 CE. About 1136 is a modern estimate, not a date given by Giles: Giles's dedication addresses Robert, Earl of Gloucester (Book I, chapter 1, page 1); Reeve and Wright, The History of the Kings of Britain, introduction (2024), say the transmitted work was finished between Alexander's consecration as bishop of Lincoln in 1123 and January 1139, when Robert of Torigni showed Henry of Huntingdon a copy at Bec (https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/history-of-the-kings-of-britain/introduction/A8233452C6A6E3E4FBCCE1E3A87D38C3, read 14 October 2026, 7:02am); Wikipedia, Historia Regum Britanniae, read 11 October 2026, 8:54pm, calls it written around 1136.

The text is the work itself.

## Editions

Giles, J. A., ed. *Galfredi Monumetensis Historia Britonum, nunc primum in Anglia, novem codd. msstis collatis*. London: D. Nutt, 1844.

Free to use: Published in London in 1844, before 1931, so free in the United States; Giles died in 1884, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1954. The scan is the National Central Library of Florence's copy, scanned by Google.

Free to use: Published in Halle in 1854, before 1931, so free in the United States; Albert Schulz died in 1893, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1963.
