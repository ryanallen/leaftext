# The Life of Gildas

Written in Latin, 1100–1165 CE. Its closing line names its author, Caradoc of Llancarfan, as Mommsen's two manuscripts do; Mommsen, introduction pages 3–4, dates it to the twelfth century, and one of its copies, Durham B. II. 35, was written in 1166. Scholars' estimate; the text gives no date.

The text is the work itself.

## Editions

Mommsen, Theodor, ed. *Chronica minora saec. IV. V. VI. VII.* Vol. 3. With indices by Johannes Lucas. Monumenta Germaniae Historica, Auctores antiquissimi 13. Berlin: Weidmann, 1898.

Free to use: Published in Berlin in 1898, before 1931, so free in the United States; Mommsen died in 1903, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1973. Johannes Lucas, who compiled the indices, is taken to be the Berlin classical scholar Ludwig Johannes Lucas, born in 1865, whom the German National Library's record GND 1075057418 gives as dying on 10 March 1939, so his term ran out at the end of 2009; its other record, GND 1055299599, gives only the birth year 1865. The scan is the University of Florida's copy. The volume holds the History of the Britons and Gildas's De excidio too, which have their own rows.
