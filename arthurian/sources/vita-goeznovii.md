# The Saints Who Meet Arthur

Written in Latin, 1199 CE. The preface on printed p. 91 claims 1019, but the Eudo of Léon it addresses is attested in 1179, and Guillotel read its date as MCXCIX, 1199 (Guigon, review of Bourgès, Bulletin de la Société d'histoire et d'archéologie de Bretagne, 2021, p. 461). Title page, retained PDF p. 13, reads Paris, H. Champion; Londres, Bernard Quaritch, MDCCCLXXXIII.

The text is the work itself.
