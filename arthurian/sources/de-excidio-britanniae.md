# On the Ruin of Britain

Written in Latin, 540–547 CE. Gildas says he writes in the forty-fourth year from the siege of Mount Badon, which was the year of his birth; Mommsen, introduction pages 8–9, following Zimmer, puts his birth a few years before 504, Badon about 500, and the letter not many years before 547. Scholars' estimate; the text gives no date.

The text is the work itself.

## Editions

Mommsen, Theodor, ed. *Chronica minora saec. IV. V. VI. VII.* Vol. 3. With indices by Johannes Lucas. Monumenta Germaniae Historica, Auctores antiquissimi 13. Berlin: Weidmann, 1898.

Free to use: Published in Berlin in 1898, before 1931, so free in the United States; Mommsen died in 1903, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1973. Johannes Lucas, who compiled the indices, is taken to be the Berlin classical scholar Ludwig Johannes Lucas, born in 1865, whom the German National Library's record GND 1075057418 gives as dying on 10 March 1939, so his term ran out at the end of 2009; its other record, GND 1055299599, gives only the birth year 1865. The scan is the University of Florida's copy. The volume holds the History of the Britons and Caradoc's Life of Gildas too, which have their own rows.
