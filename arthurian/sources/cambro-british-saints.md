# The Saints Who Meet Arthur

Written in Latin, 1086–1140 CE. Four separate lives, oldest to newest: Lifris wrote Cadoc's shortly before 1086 (Wikipedia, "Cadoc"); Carannog's about 1100, Padarn's in the 1120s (Wikipedia, "List of Arthurian literature"); Illtud's about 1140 (Wikipedia, "Illtud"). Title page, retained PDF p. 7, reads Llandovery, William Rees, MDCCCLIII.

The text is the work itself.
