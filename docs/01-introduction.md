# Meet Leaftext

> Your AI agents, a bot of your own and every file you read, in one window on your own machine

![The Leaftext window with a Markdown document open: the app bar with the vault picker in the middle, the document's tab and the page header naming where it sits, the icon rail and the side pane holding the document's headings at left, the rendered page in the middle with the floating bar over its foot, and the minimap rail down the right edge](../imgs/leaftext-v3.png)

Leaftext is a free app for Windows and macOS. It runs your AI agents as a team, gives you a bot of your own, and turns the files you already have into pages worth reading.

- **Agents.** Run [Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity](01-features/10-agents.md) in consoles beside your files.
- **Your own bot.** The [wisp](01-features/05-settings.md#your-wisp) answers you on Slack, Discord and iMessage.
- **Every file as a page.** Markdown, web pages, data, email, Office documents and books [open as clean pages](01-features/01-rendering.md).
- **Write where you read.** [Click into a sentence and type](01-features/07-editing.md), then save when you are ready.
- **Keep your place.** [Tabs](01-features/02-navigation.md), a [minimap](01-features/04-minimap.md) and a searchable [library](01-features/03-library.md).

## Your files stay yours

There is no account, no cloud and no telemetry. Your files stay ordinary files that any other app can open, so you are never locked in. The one exception is a passage you choose to hear read aloud in an [ElevenLabs voice](01-features/05-settings.md#read-aloud). [Privacy](07-privacy.md) says exactly what stays on your computer.

## What you can do

### Read

Leaftext draws [Markdown](01-features/01-rendering.md) the way GitHub does, with diagrams, math and highlighted code. It also reads [XML](01-features/01-rendering.md#xml), [JSON and YAML](01-features/01-rendering.md#data-files-json-and-yaml), [saved emails](01-features/01-rendering.md#email-eml), [Word, Excel and PowerPoint files](01-features/01-rendering.md#office-and-opendocument-files) and [EPUB books](01-features/01-rendering.md#epub-books).

A Markdown table can become a [relational table](01-features/08-relational-tables.md): cards, filters and links to rows in other files.

### Move around

Keep documents open in [tabs](01-features/02-navigation.md#tabs), and go [back and forward](01-features/02-navigation.md#history) like a browser. Jump to a section from the [outline](01-features/02-navigation.md#outline) or the [minimap](01-features/04-minimap.md). Point at a link to see where it goes before you follow it.

### Find

Point Leaftext at a folder to make it a [vault](01-features/03-library.md#vaults). Then [search](01-features/03-library.md#search) it, browse it in the [library pane](01-features/03-library.md#file-tree), and see how it connects in the [graph](01-features/03-library.md#graph). You can also find [free books online](01-features/03-library.md#books-online).

### Write

[Type straight into the page](01-features/07-editing.md#inline-editing-the-reading-view), or switch to the raw source in the [code view](01-features/07-editing.md#code-view). Draw a [flowchart](01-features/07-editing.md#the-flowchart-editor) instead of typing one. Nothing is written to your file until you [save](01-features/07-editing.md#save).

### Make it yours

Pick a [theme](01-features/06-themes.md), in light or dark, and see them all first in the [gallery](https://leaftext.com/gallery.html). Every choice is kept in a [settings file](01-features/05-settings.md#files) you can read.

## Words you meet here

Underlined words such as [minimap](GLOSSARY.md#minimap) open their entry from the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md) in a sheet over the page, so you never lose your place.
