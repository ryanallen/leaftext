# Privacy

> What Leaftext reads, where it keeps it, and who it talks to

Leaftext has no account and no server. Nothing you open, write or connect is ever sent to the maker of the app.

## What stays on your computer

Every document you open is read from your own disk. Your settings, recent files, reading progress and vaults are kept in the app's own folders on your computer, and nowhere else. Leaftext collects no analytics and sends no usage reports.

## Who the app talks to

- **GitHub, for updates.** On launch the app asks the public Leaftext repository which version is newest. It downloads an installer only when you press Update.
- **A web page you open.** A web page tab loads that page the way a browser does.
- **A voice you choose.** [Read aloud](01-features/05-settings.md#read-aloud) with a computer voice stays on your machine. An ElevenLabs voice sends the passage you chose, and only after you press Continue.
- **Your wisp's channels.** Once you connect [your wisp](01-features/05-settings.md#your-wisp) to Slack or Discord, it reads and posts there with a key the app keeps.

## Google Drive

Leaftext never signs in to Google. If the Google Drive app is installed, your My Drive folder appears as an ordinary folder, and the Drive app does its own syncing.

A Google Docs, Sheets or Slides shortcut opens its document in your browser. The document is opened by Google, not fetched into Leaftext.
