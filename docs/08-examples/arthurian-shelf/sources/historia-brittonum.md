# The History of the Britons

Written in Latin, 829–830. Chapter 16 counts its own year as the fourth of King Merfyn of Gwynedd, "annus Mermini regis quartus", and Merfyn began to reign in 825; Mommsen dates Harley MS 3859, the copy his text follows, to the eleventh century.

The text is the work itself.

## Editions

Mommsen, Theodor, ed. *Chronica minora saec. IV. V. VI. VII.* Vol. 3. With indices by Johannes Lucas. Monumenta Germaniae Historica, Auctores antiquissimi 13. Berlin: Weidmann, 1898.

Free to use: Published in Berlin in 1898, before 1931, so free in the United States; Mommsen died in 1903, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1973. Johannes Lucas, who compiled the indices, is taken to be the Berlin classical scholar Ludwig Johannes Lucas, born in 1865, whom the German National Library's record GND 1075057418 gives as dying on 10 March 1939, so his term ran out at the end of 2009; its other record, GND 1055299599, gives only the birth year 1865. The scan is the University of Florida's copy.

Stevenson, Joseph, ed. *Nennii Historia Britonum ad fidem codicum manuscriptorum recensuit Josephus Stevenson*. London: English Historical Society, 1838.

Free to use: Published in London in 1838, before 1931, so free in the United States; Stevenson died in 1895, so the life-plus-seventy term ran out at the end of 1965. Read only as the second reading of a line Mommsen's scan leaves in doubt.
