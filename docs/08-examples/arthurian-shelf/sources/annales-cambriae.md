# The Welsh Annals

Written in Latin, second half of the tenth century. Phillimore, page 144: the years are written down to 977, though the last event recorded is the death of Rhodri ab Hywel Dda in 954; the annals are copied into Harley MS 3859 among the appendices of the Historia Brittonum.

The text is the work itself.
