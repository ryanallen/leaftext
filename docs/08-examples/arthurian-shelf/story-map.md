# The whole story

Every episode of the Arthurian legend the published books tell, in the story's own order, from Britain's founding to after Camlann. A note in each book links the episode its passage belongs to, and where Malory tells the episode too, his chapters are named here and linked to the map of his books.

## Before Arthur: Britain founded and the Grail brought west

<a id="the-ages-of-the-world-are-counted"></a>

### The ages of the world are counted from Adam to Judgment Day

The years from the creation to the Flood, Abraham, Moses, David, the exile in Babylon and Christ are counted, and the six ages of the world are set from Adam to the Judgment.

<a id="the-first-peoples-settle-britain-and-ireland"></a>

### Brutus, the Picts and the Irish come to settle Britain and Ireland

Britain is described; Brutus, of Aeneas's line, kills his father by accident, is driven from Italy and settles the island named for him; the Picts take the north, and the Irish come from Spain to Ireland and parts of Britain.

<a id="the-romans-rule-britain"></a>

### The Romans rule Britain from Julius Caesar until they leave

Julius Caesar takes Britain at the third try, and emperor after emperor rules it, from Claudius and Severus to Maximus, who takes its soldiers to Brittany, until the Britons kill Rome's leaders and Rome comes only to help and to punish.

<a id="the-cities-of-britain-are-named"></a>

### The twenty-eight cities of Britain are named

The history names Britain's twenty-eight walled towns, each a Cair, from Vortigern's fort to London, York and the City of the Legion.

<a id="the-devils-plan-a-prophet"></a>

### Christ empties hell, and the devils plan a prophet of their own

After Christ harrows hell, the devils meet in council, fear that baptism and confession will take every soul from them, and plan to father a child on a woman, a prophet with all their knowledge.

<a id="a-devil-ruins-a-rich-mans-family"></a>

### A devil ruins a rich man's family and leads two daughters astray

A devil kills a rich man's herds and family until he dies in despair, gets one daughter condemned and buried alive, and sends a woman to lead the youngest into a life with men, while a holy man counsels the eldest.

<a id="a-devil-fathers-a-child-on-the-eldest-daughter"></a>

### A devil fathers a child on the eldest daughter as she sleeps

The eldest sister goes to bed in anger without her prayers, a devil lies with her in her sleep, and she wakes to a shut door and knows she's been tricked.

## Merlin, Uther and Arthur's birth

<a id="the-mother-confesses-and-is-held-in-a-tower"></a>

### The mother confesses to Blaise, and the judges lock her in a tower

She tells the holy man Blaise what happened, takes his penance, and when her pregnancy shows and she can name no father, the judges arrest her and Blaise has her kept in a tower.

<a id="merlin-is-born"></a>

### Merlin is born and speaks for his mother at her trial

Merlin is born hairy and is baptized at once; at a few months old he promises his mother she won't die, speaks for her before the judges, and shames the judge with the truth about his own father.

<a id="blaise-writes-merlins-book"></a>

### Blaise agrees to write Merlin's book of Joseph and the Grail

Merlin tells Blaise what he is and has him write down the story of Joseph and the Grail and all Merlin will tell him, a book to stand beside the books of the Grail keepers.

<a id="vortigern-takes-the-throne"></a>

### Vortigern has King Moine killed and takes the throne

The steward Vortigern holds back from the war, twelve barons murder the young King Moine for him, he's made king, and Constans's two younger sons flee overseas.

<a id="hengist-and-horsa-land-on-thanet"></a>

### Vortigern takes in Hengist and Horsa and gives them Thanet

Vortigern, ruling in fear of the Picts, the Irish, Rome and Ambrosius, takes in Hengist and Horsa, exiles from Germany with three ships, and gives them the island of Thanet.

<a id="germanus-makes-a-servant-a-king"></a>

### A cruel king shuts out Germanus, who blesses the servant who takes him in

Saint Germanus comes to preach in Britain; the cruel King Benli shuts him out, a servant takes him in, fire from heaven burns the king's fort, and Germanus makes the servant the first king of Powys.

<a id="vortigern-marries-hengists-daughter"></a>

### Vortigern marries Hengist's daughter and gives the Saxons Kent

The Saxons on Thanet outgrow what the Britons can give them, Hengist sends home for more warriors and his daughter, Vortigern falls in love with her at a feast and gives Kent for her, and Octha and Ebissa take lands in the north.

<a id="germanus-rebukes-vortigern"></a>

### Germanus comes to rebuke Vortigern for marrying his own daughter

Vortigern has a son by his own daughter and has her name Germanus as the father in front of the clergy; the boy asks Vortigern to cut his hair, and Germanus and the Britons curse the king.

<a id="vortigerns-tower-keeps-falling"></a>

### Vortigern's tower keeps falling, and his clerks blame a fatherless child

Vortigern kills the murderers, marries a Saxon and builds a tower that falls each night, and his star-reading clerks, who have each seen their death at a fatherless child's hand, say the tower needs that child's blood.

<a id="the-messengers-bring-merlin-to-vortigern"></a>

### Vortigern's messengers find Merlin and bring him to the king

The messengers find the boy Merlin, who knows why they've come; he sends Blaise to Northumberland, leaves his mother, and laughs on the road at a peasant's new shoes and a child's funeral, whose meaning the messengers later find true.

<a id="merlin-shows-vortigern-the-dragons"></a>

### Merlin shows Vortigern the two dragons under his tower

Merlin shames the clerks, has the water under the tower drained to show a red and a white dragon, who fight until one is killed, spares the clerks, and tells Vortigern how his reign will end.

<a id="vortimer-fights-the-saxons"></a>

### Vortimer fights the Saxons in four battles

Vortigern's son Vortimer shuts the Saxons up on Thanet three times and fights them in four battles, where Horsa falls, and dies asking to be buried at their harbor, which his people ignore.

<a id="hengists-men-murder-the-british-lords"></a>

### Hengist's men murder Vortigern's lords at a peace meeting

The Saxons come back in force, ask for peace, and at the meeting Hengist's men pull knives from their shoes and kill Vortigern's three hundred elders, taking Vortigern and Essex and Sussex for his ransom.

<a id="vortigern-flees-from-germanus"></a>

### Vortigern flees from Germanus from one fort to another

Germanus follows Vortigern to Gwrtheyrnion and then to his fort by the Teifi, where fire from heaven falls on it; others say he wandered hated until his heart burst, and his sons and his line are named.

<a id="patrick-preaches-to-the-irish"></a>

### Patrick is sent to Ireland and preaches to the Irish

After Vortigern's death Germanus goes home, and Patrick, once a captive among the Irish, studies in Rome and is sent to preach to the Irish for forty years.

<a id="pope-leo-changes-easter"></a>

### Pope Leo changes the date of Easter, and Brigid is born

The Welsh annals open in the years after Patrick's mission: Pope Leo moves Easter to a Sunday in 453 CE, Brigid is born, Patrick dies, and so do Bishops Benignus and Ebur.

<a id="pendragon-is-made-king"></a>

### Constans's sons land, and Pendragon is made king

Merlin goes back to Blaise, Constans's sons land at Winchester, and the elder, Pendragon, is made king and besieges Hengist, where he hears of Merlin.

<a id="merlin-comes-to-pendragon-in-disguise"></a>

### Merlin comes to Pendragon's men in one disguise after another

As a woodcutter, a herdsman and other strangers, Merlin tells Pendragon's men where to find him and how Uther killed Hengist, then shows them his own shape.

<a id="merlin-agrees-to-serve-the-brothers"></a>

### Merlin tricks Uther, and the brothers beg him to stay

Merlin fools Uther in one disguise after another, Pendragon teases his brother, and when Merlin proves who he is, the two beg him to stay and he agrees.

<a id="a-jealous-baron-tests-merlin"></a>

### Merlin makes peace with the Saxons, and a jealous baron tests him

Merlin counsels peace with the Saxons left in the land, and a jealous baron, in three disguises, has him foretell three deaths for one man, which all come true at a wooden bridge; after that the court writes down his prophecies, and he speaks in riddles.

<a id="the-battle-of-salisbury"></a>

### The brothers fight the Saxons at Salisbury

Merlin tells how to beat the returning Saxons and that one brother won't survive, a red dragon flies over the field, Pendragon falls, and Uther buries the dead and is made king.

<a id="the-stones-of-salisbury"></a>

### Merlin brings stones from Ireland and raises them at Salisbury

Uther takes his brother's name, and Merlin has great stones brought from Ireland and raises them over the dead at Salisbury, then tells Uther where his power comes from.

<a id="the-round-table"></a>

### Merlin makes Uther the Round Table, with one empty seat

Merlin tells Uther of the table of the Last Supper and Joseph's table, makes a third, seats fifty men at it, and says the empty place will wait for one man.

<a id="uther-falls-in-love-with-igraine"></a>

### Uther falls in love with Igraine and makes war on her husband

At a Christmas court Uther falls in love with Igraine, the duke of Tintagel's wife; she tells her husband, they leave by night, and Uther makes war on the duke and lays his land waste.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 1](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="merlin-brings-uther-to-igraine"></a>

### Merlin brings Uther to Igraine in her husband's shape

Merlin gives Uther the duke's likeness for a night at Tintagel on a promise of the child, the duke is killed, peace is made through the barons, Uther marries Igraine, and King Lot marries her daughter.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 2](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="arthur-is-born-and-given-to-ector"></a>

### Arthur is born and given to Ector to raise

Igraine tells Uther of the night she can't explain, he asks her to give up the child, and Merlin carries the newborn to Ector, who raises him as his own beside his son Kay.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 3](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="uther-is-carried-into-his-last-battle"></a>

### Uther, sick, is carried into a last battle with the Saxons

The Saxons beat Uther's army while he lies sick, Merlin has him carried into battle on a stretcher, and Uther gives away his treasure and speaks with Merlin as he dies.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 4](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="the-sword-in-the-stone"></a>

### A sword appears in a stone, and Arthur draws it for Kay

Uther is buried with no heir named, a sword appears in a stone before the church at Christmas, the barons fail to move it, and the boy Arthur pulls it out to bring Kay a sword.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 5](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="the-barons-put-off-arthur"></a>

### The barons put off choosing Arthur from feast to feast

Ector learns the truth and asks that Kay be made steward, and the barons, unwilling to take a boy king, make Arthur draw the sword again at Candlemas, Easter and Pentecost.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 6](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

<a id="arthur-is-crowned"></a>

### Arthur is knighted and crowned at Pentecost

Arthur gives away every gift the barons offer to test him, is knighted by the archbishop, lifts the sword once more and is crowned king.

Malory tells this in [Book I, chapter 7](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

## Arthur's early reign

<a id="arthurs-twelve-battles"></a>

### Arthur leads the Britons' kings in twelve battles against the Saxons

As the Saxons grow in Britain and Hengist's son Octha settles in Kent, Arthur leads the British kings in twelve battles, carries an image of Mary at Guinnion and strikes down nine hundred sixty men alone at Mount Badon, while the Saxons send to Germany for more kings.

<a id="britains-strange-sights"></a>

### Strange sights of Britain include Arthur's dog's footprint and his son's grave

The history lists Britain's and Ireland's strange sights, from Loch Lomond's sixty islands and the Severn's fighting waves to a stone at Builth printed by Arthur's dog Cavall while hunting the boar Troynt, and the grave of Arthur's son Anir, which changes length each time it's measured.

## Arthur's end

<a id="the-last-battle"></a>

### Arthur fights Mordred's host and is carried to Avalon

Gawain's ghost warns Arthur in a dream, an adder at the parley starts the battle, Arthur and Mordred strike each other down, Excalibur goes back to the water, and three queens take Arthur away.

Malory tells this in [Book XXI, chapters 3–5](../malory-sources/malory-map.tsv).

## After Camlann

<a id="the-english-kings-families-are-traced"></a>

### The English kings' families are traced back to Woden

The history traces the kings of Bernicia, Kent, East Anglia, Mercia and Deira back to Woden, down to the Northumbrian Ecgfrith, who falls fighting the Picts.

<a id="the-britons-fight-the-english-kings"></a>

### British poets sing, and Urien fights the English kings in the north

Aneirin and Taliesin win fame, Maelgwn rules Gwynedd, Urien besieges the English on Lindisfarne before a rival has him killed, and Oswald, Oswiu and Penda fight the Britons and each other.

<a id="a-great-plague-and-the-battle-of-arfderydd"></a>

### A great plague comes, and the northern Britons fight at Arfderydd

The Welsh annals keep the years after Camlann to 613 CE: a great plague takes Maelgwn of Gwynedd, the northern Britons fight at Arfderydd, Gildas, Columba and Kentigern die, Augustine and Mellitus bring the English to Christ, and armies meet at Chester.

<a id="the-britons-fight-the-northumbrians-and-the-mercians"></a>

### Cadwallon and Penda fight the Northumbrians, and the Britons fight the Mercians

The Welsh annals keep the years from 614 to 799 CE: Cadwallon kills Edwin and falls at Heavenfield, Oswald and Penda fall, a second Badon is fought, plague comes back, and Offa of Mercia lays the Britons waste.

<a id="vikings-raid-wales"></a>

### Vikings raid Wales, and the annals run on to Hywel's son Rhodri

The Welsh annals keep the years from 800 to 977 CE: kings of Gwynedd, Powys and Dyfed die, heathens raid Anglesey, St Davids and York, the Saxons kill Rhodri, Hywel goes to Rome, Brunanburh is fought, and Hywel's son Rhodri dies.
