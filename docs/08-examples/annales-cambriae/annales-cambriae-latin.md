# The Welsh Annals

*Annales Cambriae*, text established by [Ryan Allen](annales-cambriae-how-it-was-made.md#ryan-allen)

## About this text

The Latin *Welsh Annals*, the years 444 to 977, as Egerton Phillimore printed them letter for letter in 1888 from their one copy, London, British Library, Harley MS 3859, established page by page against a scan of his edition.
